Kashi Vishwanath temple timings: aarti and darshan

The daily schedule as published by the temple trust, with practical tips from our host for planning your darshan.

Daily aarti schedule

AartiTime
Temple opens2:30 am
Mangala Aarti (ticketed)3:00 – 4:00 am
Midday Bhog Aarti11:30 am – 12:00 noon
Sapta Rishi Aarti7:00 – 8:30 pm
Shringar / Bhog Aartifrom 9:00 pm
Shayan Aartifrom 10:30 pm
Temple closes11:00 pm

General darshan hours

General darshan runs from 4:00 to 11:00 am, then 12:00 noon to 7:00 pm, and again after the Sapta Rishi Aarti until about 9:00 pm. Timings change on festivals such as Maha Shivratri, Sawan Mondays and Dev Deepawali, so check the official temple website the day before.

Tips from our host

  • Weekday mornings are usually calmer than evenings and weekends.
  • Phones, bags and leather items are not allowed inside; use the lockers or leave them in your room.
  • Book Mangala Aarti and special darshan tickets on the official temple website, well in advance for festivals.
  • From our colony in Kamachha the temple area is about 15–20 minutes by cab; expect a short walk near the gates.
  • For a 4 am start, a room with a kitchen means you can make tea before you leave.

Source: schedule published by Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust (shrikashivishwanath.org). Checked November 2026.

Questions

What time does Kashi Vishwanath temple open?

The temple opens at 2:30 am. Mangala Aarti is from 3 to 4 am and general darshan starts at 4 am.

What time does Kashi Vishwanath temple close?

The temple closes at 11 pm, after the Shayan Aarti that starts at 10:30 pm.

What is the best time for darshan?

Weekday mornings after 4 am are usually calmer than evenings and weekends.

Can I take my phone inside?

No. Phones and bags are not allowed inside the temple; use the lockers near the gates.
