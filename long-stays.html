Who this is for

Many guests stay in Varanasi for a week or more: for work, study, family visits or slow travel. For a long stay, a hotel room without a kitchen gets tiring and expensive. Our rooms are set up for longer stays.

Why guests stay with us for longer

  • Your own kitchen. Rooms 1, 3, 4 and 6 have a private kitchen and Room 5 has a kitchenette with induction, kettle and utensils, so you can cook simple home food every day.
  • A quiet residential colony. No crowds or late-night noise outside your door.
  • Ground-floor rooms. Easy for elders, with no stairs to climb.
  • Practical basics. Wi-Fi in every room, a geyser in each private bathroom and inverter power backup.
  • Local help. Your host is a Varanasi local and can point you to groceries, pharmacies, chai and veg restaurants.

Long-stay discounts

The discount applies automatically when you book on Airbnb: 10% off stays of 7 nights or more and 11% off stays of 28 nights or more. Every price is for 2 guests; Rooms 3 and 4 take up to 4 guests and the other rooms up to 3, with an extra charge for each additional person.

Which room for a long stay?

  • Room 4, our most spacious room with a desk nook: good for families or working stays.
  • Room 6: a larger bathroom and kitchen, good for longer stays.
  • Room 3: our largest suite, with a king bed and a projector.
  • Room 5 or Room 1: smaller and good value for one or two people.

House rules: only booked guests stay, no outside visitors; quiet hours 10 pm to 7 am; guests under 18 only with a parent or guardian.

Questions

Is there a discount for long stays?

Yes. Stays of 7 nights or more get 10% off and stays of 28 nights or more get 11% off. The discount applies automatically on Airbnb.

Can we cook our own food?

Yes. Rooms 1, 3, 4 and 6 have a private kitchen and Room 5 has a kitchenette. Room 2 has no kitchen.

Are the rooms suitable for elderly guests?

All rooms are on the ground floor with a private bathroom, so there are no stairs to climb.
