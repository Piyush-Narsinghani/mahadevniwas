About the Budget Fan Room

The Budget Fan Room is our lowest-priced room. It has a 6×6 ft bed, ceiling fan, a desk and chair, a kettle with cups, and a private bathroom with a geyser. There is no AC and no kitchen.

It suits budget travellers and students who want a clean private room and bathroom rather than a dorm, in winter and the cooler months especially.

Where you are

Mahadev Niwas is a family-run homestay in a quiet residential colony in Kamachha, Varanasi. Assi Ghat is about 2.5 km by road, and the ghats are 15–20 minutes away by cab or scooty. The road is car-accessible up to the gate, with free street parking in the colony and paid parking next door if you need it.

House rules in short

Questions

How many guests can stay in the Budget Fan Room?

The price is for 2 guests. One extra person can stay at an extra charge, so the room takes up to 3.

Does the budget room have AC?

No. It is a fan room. If you need AC, the Compact AC Room is our best-value AC option.
