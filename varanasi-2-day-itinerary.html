A 2-day Varanasi itinerary from a local host

The plan we share with guests: ghats and Kashi Vishwanath on day one, temples and Sarnath on day two.

This is the plan our host shares most often with guests staying in Kamachha. It mixes the big sights with quieter corners. Timings change on festival days, so check with your host the evening before.

Day 1: The ghats and Kashi Vishwanath

Early morning: Assi Ghat

Start early for the morning aarti at Assi Ghat (around 5:30 am). Then walk along the ghats as the city wakes up; the light on the river is at its best in the first hour.

Late morning: Kashi Vishwanath Temple

The temple opens at 2:30 am and Mangala Aarti runs from 3 to 4 am. General darshan is from 4 to 11 am, 12 noon to 7 pm, and again after the Sapta Rishi Aarti until about 9 pm. See our full Kashi Vishwanath timings. Weekday mornings are usually calmer than evenings and weekends. Leave phones and bags as instructed at the entry, and allow extra time for queues.

Afternoon: lanes and lunch

Walk the old lanes near the temple, try a classic Banarasi breakfast or lunch, and come back to your room to rest through the hottest part of the day.

Evening: Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh

The evening aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat starts around 5:45 pm in winter (Nov–Jan) and around 6:45 pm in summer (Apr–Sep). Reach early for a good spot.

Day 2: Temples, Sarnath and Banarasi silk

Morning: Durga Kund and Sankat Mochan

Two of the city's best-known temples. Go early, before the day's crowds.

Afternoon: Sarnath

Sarnath, where the Buddha gave his first sermon, is north of the city. Allow two to three hours to see the stupa, ruins and museum, plus travel time. The museum is closed on Fridays. See our Sarnath half-day guide.

Evening: Banarasi silk and a slow walk

Browse Banarasi silk, then end the day sitting on the ghat steps as the lamps come on. Or cook a simple dinner in your room's kitchen and rest before your onward journey.

Questions

How many days do I need in Varanasi?

Two full days cover the ghats, Kashi Vishwanath, the evening aarti, the temples and Sarnath at a comfortable pace. Add a third day if you want to slow down: see our 3-day itinerary.

What time is the Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh?

Around 5:45 pm in winter (November to January) and around 6:45 pm in summer (April to September). Timings change on festivals, so check the day before.
