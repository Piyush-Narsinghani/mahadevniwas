Questions

How far are the ghats and Kashi Vishwanath?

Assi Ghat is about 2.5 km, Kashi Vishwanath Temple about 3 km and BHU about 3.1 km by road: 15 to 20 minutes by auto. Sankat Mochan and Durga temples are closer.

Is smoking allowed?

Yes, smoking is allowed inside the rooms. Please use the ashtray and keep the room clean.

Do the rooms have a kitchen?

Rooms 1, 3, 4 and 6 have a private kitchen and Room 5 has a kitchenette. Room 2 has no kitchen.

What are the check-in and checkout times?

Self check-in from 12:00 noon. Checkout by 10:00 am. Late checkout needs an extra night booked.

How many guests does the price include?

Every price is for 2 guests. Rooms 3 and 4 take up to 4 guests and the other rooms up to 3, with an extra charge for each additional person.

Can I get an extra mattress?

Yes, an extra mattress is available for an additional charge. Ask on WhatsApp before you arrive.

How do I book?

Every room is booked through Airbnb. Pick a room above and tap Check dates on Airbnb. For questions, message us on WhatsApp.
