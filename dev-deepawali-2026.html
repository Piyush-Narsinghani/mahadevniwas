What Dev Deepawali is

Dev Deepawali, the "Diwali of the gods", is celebrated in Varanasi on Kartik Purnima, the full-moon night fifteen days after Diwali. In 2026 it falls on Tuesday, 24 November. In the evening the ghats along the Ganga are lit with lakhs of clay diyas and special aartis fill the riverfront.

It is the busiest night of the year in the city, so a little planning makes a big difference.

How to plan the day

  1. Book your stay early. The nights around 24 November fill first, and many guests stay two or three nights so they are not rushing on the day itself.
  2. Head out by early afternoon. Traffic near the ghats is usually restricted that evening, so expect to walk the last stretch. Comfortable footwear helps.
  3. Choose one stretch of ghats. Trying to cover everything in the crowd is tiring. Pick an area, settle in before sunset, and stay put.
  4. Keep valuables minimal. Carry a small bag, some cash, a charged phone and a power bank.
  5. Come back to quiet. After the crowds, it helps to return to a calm room away from the ghat noise.

Questions

When is Dev Deepawali in 2026?

Dev Deepawali 2026 is on Tuesday, 24 November, the night of Kartik Purnima.

How far is Mahadev Niwas from the ghats?

We are in a quiet colony in Kamachha, 15 to 20 minutes from the ghats. On Dev Deepawali evening expect traffic restrictions near the ghats and some walking.

What time do the diyas light up on Dev Deepawali?

The ghats are lit from around sunset, and the main celebrations continue through the evening. Reach your spot before sunset.

Which nights should I book for Dev Deepawali 2026?

Book the night of 24 November, and ideally 23 or 25 November too, so you are not travelling on the busiest day.
